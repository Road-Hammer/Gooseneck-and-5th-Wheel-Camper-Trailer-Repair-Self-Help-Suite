# Plumbing Troubleshooting — Full-Size Flagstaff LQ (Test Model)

## Test model

Same **test coach** as the electrical guide:

**Full-size Forest River Flagstaff** living-quarters travel trailer  
(kitchen + bath + fresh/gray/black systems; examples: Flagstaff Super Lite / Classic-class full-size floorplans).

This is a **worked example**. Your wet-bay valve labels and pump location will match **your** Flagstaff manual, not every Flagstaff ever built.

**Separate section:** electrical troubleshooting (including mice on wires) is a **different guide** — do not mix live 120 V work with wet plumbing.

---

## Safety

- Sewage = biohazard (gloves, wash up).  
- Kill **water heater** power/gas before draining heater.  
- Standing water + **120 V outlets** → stop; dry and de-energize first.  
- Mice debris near plumbing may include **chewed PEX and electrical** — handle both systems carefully.

---

## Symptom → first checks (Flagstaff LQ)

### No water anywhere

| Check | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Mode | City water hose on vs tank + pump switch |
| Tank | Fresh tank empty or fill valve closed |
| Pump | Fuse, 12 V at pump, strainer clogged |
| Winterize | Still full of antifreeze / valves left on winterize path |
| Freeze | Lines frozen solid |

See also: [fresh-water-pump-city-water-leaks](/guide/fresh-water-pump-city-water-leaks).

### Pump runs continuously

1. Faucet or toilet valve not seating.  
2. Leak on pressurized side (listen at night).  
3. Failed pump pressure switch.  
4. Empty tank / sucking air.

### Water only on city, not from tank (or reverse)

- Check valve stuck.  
- Pump not priming.  
- Tank outlet hose collapsed.  
- City inlet check valve leaking back.

### No hot water

1. Heater mode (electric / LP / both) as equipped.  
2. Bypass valves still in **winterize bypass**.  
3. Element or LP issues → appliance manual / pro.  
4. Air in lines after dry storage — purge.

### Toilet will not flush / weak flush

1. Black tank full or valve not fully closed for flush water.  
2. Toilet valve / seal (Thetford/Dometic style per unit).  
3. No fresh water pressure (pump/city).  
4. Clog — careful; pro if tool work needed.

### Will not dump / slow dump

1. Pull valve not fully open.  
2. Frozen valve.  
3. Cap still on (embarrassing but real).  
4. Tank flush stuck (if Flagstaff floorplan has black-tank flush — common on many Forest River towables; use per manual).  

### Sewage smell inside

1. Black tank valve not sealed.  
2. Toilet seal dry (add water).  
3. Roof vent blocked (nest!).  
4. P-trap dry after storage — run water or antifreeze in traps.

### Wet floor / ceiling stain

1. Fresh fitting under sink.  
2. Water heater relief or fittings.  
3. Tub surround / shower pan (not always “pipes”).  
4. Roof leak misread as plumbing.  
5. **Mice-chewed PEX** in belly or wall.

## Mice and plumbing (Flagstaff-class)

Rodents often share the **same chases** as water lines.

| Clue | Action |
|------|--------|
| Droppings in wet bay / under sinks | Inspect all soft tubing and nearby 12 V wires |
| Chewed PEX or vinyl | Depressurize; replace damaged section with correct fittings |
| Nest in underbelly | Do not crush nests into ducts; clean safely; repair lines |
| Soft floor near bath | Suspect long-term leak + possible wire damage |

After any rodent plumbing repair, run the **electrical** mouse checklist in the sister guide.

## Ordered troubleshooting flow (use this order)

```
1. Identify mode: city vs pump vs winterize valves
2. Confirm water present in tank / hose
3. Look for active wet spots (safety)
4. Pump power and fuse (12 V only after dry footing)
5. Branch isolation: kitchen vs bath vs outdoor shower
6. Tanks/valves if dump or odor issue
7. Rodent inspection if storage or intermittent damage
```

## Stop conditions

- Active sewage in living space  
- Unknown chemical in fresh tank  
- Combined electrical short and wet floor  

## Pro help triggers

- Tank replacement  
- Major underbelly re-pipe  
- Persistent black-tank odor after proper process  
- Soft structural floor
